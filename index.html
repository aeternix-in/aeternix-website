A claim can lose money at four points. We work all four.

Where it leaks

What we do

By-product

1

Pre-authorisation

The insurer must decide within one hour.

Where it leaksRequests go in thin. They are approved short or denied, and enhancements are raised late.

What we doWe build each request from the clinical notes, with the evidence a reviewer looks for, and our clinical team checks it.

2

Discharge and filing

Final approval is due within three hours.

Where it leaksA missing report, a wrong code, a non-payable item, a charge above the agreed tariff.

What we doWe audit the bill and the file against the policy, the tariff and IRDAI’s lists before anything is sent.

3

Insurer queries

While a query waits, the patient waits.

Where it leaksReplies go late or incomplete, and an unanswered query turns into a cut.

What we doA draft reply is assembled from the medical record in minutes. A doctor approves it before it goes.

4

Settlement

The money arrives later, often short.

Where it leaksNo one matches the payment to the bill. Short payments are written off.

What we doWe match every payment to its bank credit, mark each cut as the policy’s or the hospital’s, and dispute the wrong ones.

By-productEvery step produces FHIR R4 claim data, so your claims are ready for NHCX.

Across every claim, reports by insurer and TPA show what each one cuts and why. That is the evidence for your next tariff negotiation.

NHCX-ready, as a by-product.

The National Health Claims Exchange, run by the National Health Authority, carries claims as FHIR R4 bundles defined in the ABDM implementation guide published by NRCeS. Every claim we prepare is structured that way from the first step.

When your insurers move to the exchange, your claims are already in its format. There is no second project.

Questions

Is NHCX mandatory for hospitals?

Not as of October 2026. The exchange is live and insurers are connecting to it. When yours move, the claims we prepare are already in its format.

Do we have to change our hospital software?

No. We work on top of the system and documents you already have.

Does this replace our insurance desk?

No. Your desk keeps its people and its relationships with insurers and TPAs. We take the repetitive work and the follow-up off it.

Does patient data leave the hospital?

Not during the audit. No patient-identifiable data leaves the premises, and the work runs under a mutual NDA. Data handling for any later work is set out in its proposal.

What does the audit cost?

Nothing. Any work after it is priced in a written proposal.
